How to play

Driver: accelerate and brake smoothly, keep to the left lane, obey signals, indicate before entering or leaving a bus bay, stop inside the yellow stop zone and open the doors only when stationary.

Keyboard: W/↑ accelerate, S/↓ brake, A/← and D/→ steer. Q/E indicators. Space toggles doors.

Ride-Along: enable location permission while travelling. GPS movement advances the simulator without uploading your location in this prototype.
