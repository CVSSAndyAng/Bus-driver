Your real bus supplies the speed and movement. Doors appear only at bus stops. Horn and hazards stay available when the bus is stopped at lights or in traffic.

Phone + Driver Seat Controls

Phone steering: on Android or iPhone, hold the phone in landscape and rotate it left/right like a steering wheel. Tap CENTRE STEERING whenever you change your holding position. The cockpit wheel rotates with the phone.

Desktop steering: drag the cockpit wheel with a mouse or finger. A/D or arrow keys work too.

Pedals: press the cockpit accelerator and brake pedals, or use W/↑ and S/↓.

Operations: door, bell, next-stop announcement and kneeling are on the left console. Indicators, wipers and lights are on the right console.

Windscreen: choose Singapore Street View, AR live camera, or the offline training road before starting. Street View requires a Google Maps JavaScript API browser key. AR camera requires camera permission.

Driving: keep left, indicate before leaving a stop, stop smoothly, and open doors only after stopping.
