Ride-Along Mode

Use this while riding a real Singapore bus. GPS stays in this browser and drives the virtual trip progress.

Driver Seat Controls

Steering wheel: drag it directly with a mouse or finger. The bus wheel turns multiple rotations like a real heavy vehicle steering wheel. A/D or arrow keys work too.

Pedals: press the cockpit accelerator and brake pedals, or use W/↑ and S/↓.

Operations: door, bell, next-stop announcement and kneeling are on the left console. Indicators, wipers and lights are on the right console.

Driving: keep left, indicate before leaving a stop, stop smoothly, and open doors only after stopping.
