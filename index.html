How to play

Driver: hold GO to accelerate, BRAKE to slow down, steer gently, stop inside the yellow bus-stop zone, open the doors, then close them before moving.

Ride-Along: enable location permission while travelling. The virtual journey reacts to your real movement. GPS is processed in your browser and is not uploaded by this prototype.

Earn points for smooth stops, obeying speed limits, correct door use and helping passengers.
